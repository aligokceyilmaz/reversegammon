Frequently Asked Questions

What is ALVAT?

ALVAT is reverse backgammon. The board, checkers and dice are classic, but the rules are flipped: the board starts empty, you place your checkers with the dice, and instead of hitting you lock the opponent by sitting on top of a lone checker. The first to bring all 15 checkers across and bear them off wins.

How do I play?

Open the app and tap “How to Play?” in the main menu — a short illustrated guide walks you through every rule step by step.

How does online play work?

Tap Play Online and you'll be matched with another player in real time. You just need an internet connection — no account or sign-up required.

Is it free?

Yes, ALVAT is free to download and play.

How do I change the language?

Tap the 🌐 language button at the top-right of the main menu to switch between English and Turkish.
