Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BACKGAMMON - ALVAT (“ALVAT”, “we”, “the app”) handles information. We built ALVAT to collect as little as possible. We do not sell your data and we do not show third-party advertising.

Information we handle

  • On your device only: your chosen display name, avatar emoji, game statistics, selected theme, language and sound preference are stored locally on your device. This data stays on your device.
  • Anonymous ID: to enable online play, the app signs you in anonymously with Google Firebase. This creates a random identifier. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or any personal account.
  • Online matches: while you play an online game, the current game state (checker positions, dice) and the display name and avatar you chose in the app are stored temporarily so your opponent's device can show the game. Please avoid using personal information as your display name.

What we do NOT collect

  • No account, email, password or phone number.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising or tracking across other apps and websites.
  • No sale of personal data.

Third-party services

ALVAT uses Google Firebase (Anonymous Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to power online play. Google processes this data as our service provider. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

ALVAT is a board game suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Data retention

On-device data remains until you delete the app or clear its data. Online match data is temporary and tied to a game session.

Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
